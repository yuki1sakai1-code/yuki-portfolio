Fujix Co., Ltd. · Global procurement

Cut international shipping costs

RoleProcurement / International Trade Specialist
CompanyFujix Co., Ltd. (2017–2022)
OperationChina factory battery procurement & international trade
Recognition2019 Internal Improvement Contest — 3rd place

Challenge

For decades the company shipped batteries from Japan to China by air freight — expensive, but necessary given delivery deadlines and past customs friction on sea routes, which could take over two months. Batteries are classified as dangerous goods, so shipping costs were extremely high: batteries alone made up 15% of a ¥35M annual logistics bill.

Approach

I analyzed six years of customer data to predict demand timing, then used our improved customs relationships to shift from air freight to far cheaper sea shipping — sending batteries to the China factory over a month ahead of need. I validated the approach with a single trial case before scaling to full implementation.

Result

Roughly 15% reduction in shipping costs and about ¥5M in annual savings, with no change to delivery reliability. The initiative was recognized with 3rd place in the company's 2019 Internal Improvement Contest.

Challenge

Approach

I drove the Dexter → Milo migration, managing the transition across unrelated CMS architectures. I then carried the same page through the follow-on Milo → DA move — lighter, since it stayed within Adobe's ecosystem, but still requiring careful QA and stakeholder communication.

Result

Maintained page continuity and publishing quality across both transitions, with minimal disruption to the live user experience.
